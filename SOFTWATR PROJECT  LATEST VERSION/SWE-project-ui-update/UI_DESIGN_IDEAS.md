# Hotel Booking App - UI Design Concept

## 1. Global UI Design Guidelines

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Brand Color**: Cool Blue (`#1D4ED8` or similar deep, professional blue). Conveys trust, cleanliness, and business.
*   **Accent/Warm Tones**: Earthy Gold/Sand (`#D97706` or `#B45309`). Used sparingly for "Book Now" buttons, star ratings, and special highlights to add a touch of luxury.
*   **Backgrounds**: Off-white or very light grey (`#F9FAFB`) for the main app background to keep it neat and easy on the eyes. White (`#FFFFFF`) for cards and content panels to create separation.
*   **Text Colors**: Dark Charcoal (`#1F2937`) for primary text (easier to read than pure black) and medium grey (`#4B5563`) for secondary text like descriptions and dates.

### Typography
*   **Font Family**: A clean, modern sans-serif like *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Segoe UI*.
*   **Font Sizes**:
    *   **Main Headings (H1)**: 32px - 36px, Bold (e.g., "Find Your Perfect Stay").
    *   **Section Headers (H2)**: 24px, Semi-Bold (e.g., "Featured Hotels", "About Us").
    *   **Card Titles**: 18px - 20px, Medium (e.g., Hotel Names).
    *   **Body Text**: 16px, Regular (e.g., descriptions, standard text).
    *   **Small Details**: 14px, Regular (e.g., distance from city center, review counts).

### Overall Layout Style
*   **Neat & Simple**: Generous padding (whitespace) around elements. No cluttered menus.
*   **Card-Based UI**: Use slightly rounded corners (border-radius: 8px or 12px) and very soft drop shadows on hotel listings and form panels so they "pop" off the light grey background.

## 2. Home Page Tab

### Background Image Strategy
*   A high-quality, welcoming, and slightly darkened image of a specific, elegant hotel lobby or a luxury room view. The image should take up the top portion (Hero Section) of the screen.

### Panels & Layout
*   **Hero Section (Top)**:
    *   Features the background image.
    *   Centered white text: "Book Your Ideal Hotel Stay."
    *   **Search Panel**: A floating white panel overlaid on the bottom half of the background image. Keep it simple:
        *   Input 1: Destination or Hotel Name.
        *   Input 2: Check-in / Check-out Dates.
        *   Input 3: Number of Guests / Rooms.
        *   Button: A prominent "Search" button in the accent Earthy Gold color.
*   **Featured Hotels Section (Middle)**:
    *   A horizontal scrolling list or a grid of cards (like the reference screenshots).
    *   **Each Card contains**: Thumbnail image, Hotel Name, Star Rating (in gold), Location snippet, Price per night, and a clean "View Deal" text link or button.
*   **Why Book With Us / Special Offers (Lower)**:
    *   A simple row of three minimalist icons/text blocks: "Free Cancellation", "Best Price Guarantee", "24/7 Support".

## 3. About Us Page

### Layout & Panels
*   **Header Panel**: A solid Cool Blue background header that says "About Us".
*   **Main Content Card**: A large, clean white card centered on the page.
    *   **Our Story**: A short, warm paragraph explaining the mission (e.g., "We believe booking a hotel should be as relaxing as the stay itself. We focus exclusively on top-tier accommodations for both business and leisure...").
    *   **Why Choose Us?**: A bulleted list with simple icons next to each point (e.g., Quality Assured, Curated Selections, Transparent Pricing).
    *   **Team / Image**: A single, wide image inside the card showing a welcoming concierge desk or a pristine hotel room, reinforcing the luxury/business vibe.

---

## 4. Contact Us Page

### Layout & Panels
*   **Header Panel**: Matches the About Us page, titled "Contact Us".
*   **Split Layout (Two Panels side-by-side or stacked on smaller screens)**:
    *   **Left Panel (Get in Touch)**:
        *   A clean, simple form inside a white card.
        *   Fields: "Your Name" (text input), "Email Address" (text input), "Subject" (dropdown: Booking Issue, General Inquiry, Business Partnership), and "Message" (text area).
        *   Button: A Cool Blue "Send Message" button.
    *   **Right Panel (Contact Details)**:
        *   A slightly smaller card with a soft grey background.
        *   **Customer Support**: A phone number icon and the number (e.g., "1-800-HOTELS").
        *   **Email**: A mail icon with "support@hotelapp.com".
        *   **Business Hours**: "Available 24/7 for your convenience."
        *   Optional: A small embedded map or a neat illustration of a hotel building.
